Ambient Wall Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 Developer: Toussaint Campbell ("we", "us") · Contact: featherfluffllc@gmail.com

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Ambient Wall has no sign-up and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We don't sell or share your data with data brokers, and we don't ask to track you across other apps.
  • We don't run a database of your data. Your layouts and settings sync through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). We, the developer, can't read it.
  • Your iPhone mic is never recorded. If you turn on Room Sound, the iPhone turns sound into a few loudness numbers while the app is open and sends only those numbers to your Apple TV on your home Wi-Fi. Audio is never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Location is approximate and optional. If you allow it, the app reads your location once and rounds it to about 1 km to suggest nearby transit stops.

Services that answer the app's requests

To show live information, your Apple TV or iPhone contacts these services directly. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address and the request itself, for example which feed or which map area. The app sends no name, email, advertising ID or account ID to them. Their own privacy policies apply.

  • Apple: iCloud/CloudKit, WeatherKit, Maps, Music, HomeKit, StoreKit.
  • News feeds you choose in Headlines (each publisher's RSS/Atom feed).
  • Transit: MTA (subway feeds and Bus Time) and the Port Authority of NY & NJ (PATH times).
  • NOAA / National Weather Service: radar images on the Map tile.
  • Our transit relay (if enabled): a small server we run on Cloudflare that fetches public transit feeds and passes them to the app, as the MTA's and NJ Transit's terms require. It receives your IP address and the stop or area you asked about. It does not store request logs or build profiles.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad identifiers (no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because we don't track).
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or marketing SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of data, and no sharing with data brokers.

Your choices

  • Permissions: Location, Microphone, Calendar, Home, Apple Music and Local Network are each asked for only when you use the feature. You can turn any of them off in Settings › Privacy & Security at any time.
  • Delete your synced data: on the iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Ambient Wall and delete its data. Deleting the app removes what's stored on that device.

Children

Ambient Wall is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Security

Sync uses Apple's encrypted iCloud. iPhone-to-TV traffic on your network (Room Sound numbers) is encrypted (TLS) and works only with devices you've paired.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above, and we'll describe meaningful changes in the app's release notes.
